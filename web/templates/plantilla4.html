Human-Centered Design & Viability

User Impact & Academic Feasibility

Balancing high social value for visually impaired users with practical, rapid prototyping within the academic year.

End-User Value

Designed for Dignity & Autonomy

Visually impaired users require intuitive, non-intrusive mobility aids that enhance natural perception rather than adding cognitive overload.

  • Preserves Auditory Awareness: Tactile vibration alerts on the handle communicate danger discreetly without masking vital street sounds.
  • Psychological Confidence: Knowing upper-body space is actively monitored reduces anxiety and encourages independent urban travel.
  • Ergonomic Balance: Custom additive-manufactured grip houses battery and circuitry without straining the wrist.

Project Viability

Feasibility Within the Academic Course

This proposal was selected over alternative concepts due to its optimal balance between technological depth and realistic implementation timelines.

  • Accessible Hardware Stack: Relies on reliable, cost-effective components (Arduino microcontrollers, HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensors, coin vibration motors).
  • Rapid C++ Iteration: Modular firmware allows immediate calibration of detection thresholds and haptic patterns during field testing.
  • In-House Prototyping: 3D printing enables fast mechanical revisions without expensive external tooling.
