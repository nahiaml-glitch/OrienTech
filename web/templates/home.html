Smart assisted cane for visually impaired individuals.

Bridging the gap in spatial awareness by detecting upper-body hazards using IoT ultrasonic sensors and haptic feedback.

Project Overview & Technology

This project focuses on developing an advanced smart cane that goes beyond traditional ground-level obstacle detection. By equipping the cane with state-of-the-art IoT telemetry, ultrasonic sensors, and precise haptic feedback mechanisms, it actively scans for chest and head-level hazards (like low-hanging branches or street signs). The system processes this spatial data in real-time, sending intuitive vibration alerts to the user's hand, greatly enhancing their autonomy and safety in dynamic urban environments.

Accessible Pricing & Full Customization (€50 – €100)

With an estimated price ranging between €50 and €100, each cane can be tailored to the user's exact preferences—from choosing the rolling tip ball to selecting its smart features, adapting seamlessly from the most advanced setup to the simplest configuration.
